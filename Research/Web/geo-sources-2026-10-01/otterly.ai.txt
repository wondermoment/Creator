What is the difference between LLM Monitoring and AI Search Monitoring? LLM Monitoring focuses solely on tracking the direct output of large language models (LLMs). However, modern AI tools like ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews do more than just generate LLM-based responses—they also incorporate web search capabilities. This is why you see links and citations in their responses; these references come from external web searches rather than the LLM itself. ‍ Because of this, LLM monitoring solutions do not provide insights into link citations. OtterlyAI, however, takes a different approach by analyzing AI Search platforms directly. Think of it as a first-person perspective on how these platforms retrieve and present information. OtterlyAI scans and processes data, offering valuable insights into your brand&#x27;s visibility, sentiment analysis, and the links being referenced. Why results between a manual search might differ from OtterlyAI? If you perform a manual search on e.g. ChatGPT or Perplexity.AI you might see a different result than those in OtterlyAI. The reason for that is that those AI Search platforms use a so-called Memory RAG where they store information on you, your search history. Other information - such as location - is also used to personalise the experience. OtterlyAI on the other hand, aims to provide the most neutral, objective monitoring available.

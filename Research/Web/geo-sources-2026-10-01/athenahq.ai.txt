AEO is the practice of optimizing your brand to appear in AI-generated answers across platforms like ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity. Unlike SEO, which ranks links, AEO is two-dimensional: being mentioned matters, but how you are mentioned matters more. Gartner predicts traditional search volume will drop 25% by 2026 due to AI. AEO is how you stay visible. How is AI Search different from traditional Google search? Google ranks pages and drives clicks. AI Search synthesizes sources and delivers a direct answer, often with no click at all. Zero-click searches on Google jumped from 56% in 2024 to 69% in 2025. The signals that drive visibility in AI Search are fundamentally different from traditional SEO, and require a new approach. What does AthenaHQ do? We are the command center for AEO and GEO.
